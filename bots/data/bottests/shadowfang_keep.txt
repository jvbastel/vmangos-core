# The scripted runs of Shadowfang Keep. The format is in src/bots/RunScript.h,
# the route in routes/shadowfang_keep.txt. wowai/test-realm/runs.py starts them:
#   wowai/test-realm/runs.py --ticket <N> --script shadowfang_keep scenario three_enemies 10
script shadowfang_keep shadowfang_keep
driver Ezzra
group Grukk Hela Shanka Morzul
outside 1 1811.78 -4410.50 -18.47   # the group waits in Orgrimmar, as in ragefire_chasm
level 18

# The three-enemy pack of jvbastel/WOWAI#118 (spec #102): Rethilgore and his
# two Bleak Worgs, three elite melee enemies of level 18-20 that stand within
# 9.2 yd of each other. The miners of deadmines.txt were too easy: today's
# tank lost no threat on them. No ranged pull, no patrol, no crowd control:
# the group holds its place and the tank pulls. runs.py reports the threat
# losses of each pull, the healer's, and whether the pull was clean. 10 runs
# are one measurement.
scenario three_enemies
teleport pull_rethilgore
clear 100 rethilgore    # only the pack stays; also the patrols above
command follow
wait 20                 # the buffs
command stay
refill                  # full health and mana after the buffs
wait 3                  # a bot that drank stands up: the group rests no more
fight rethilgore
expect pack_dead rethilgore

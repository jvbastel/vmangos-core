# The scripted runs of the Deadmines. The format is in src/bots/RunScript.h,
# the route in routes/deadmines.txt. wowai/test-realm/runs.py starts them:
#   wowai/test-realm/runs.py --ticket <N> --script deadmines scenario three_enemies 10
script deadmines deadmines
driver Ezzra
group Grukk Hela Shanka Morzul
outside 1 1811.78 -4410.50 -18.47   # the group waits in Orgrimmar, as in ragefire_chasm
level 18                            # the pack is level 17-18

# The three-enemy pack of jvbastel/WOWAI#118 (spec #102): one fixed pack of
# three Defias Miners (level 17-18, melee, standing; 8 to 17 yd apart, so
# each one joins the pull). No ranged pull, no patrol, no crowd control:
# the group holds its place and the tank pulls. The result file reports the
# threat losses of the pull, the healer's threat losses, and runs.py says
# whether the pull was clean. 10 runs are one measurement.
scenario three_enemies
teleport pull_miners
clear 60 miners         # only the pack stays
command follow
wait 20                 # the buffs
command stay
refill                  # full health and mana after the buffs
wait 3                  # a bot that drank stands up: the group rests no more
fight miners
expect pack_dead miners
